Who We Are

The Audience Decides. The Artist Rises.

UNMUTE puts the audience at the heart of the music industry. We connect fans directly to the artists they believe in. No algorithm decides who gets seen — the people do. Fans become the new A&Rs, choosing who rises based on pure talent. When you fund an artist, you collect points — and as more people fund them after you, your points keep growing to unlock unreleased music, concert access, and merch.

Every artist deserves a fair chance, regardless of connections or budget.

Every contribution is traceable. Artists and fans see where the money goes.

The public is the best judge of talent.
