Who We Are

THE AUDIENCE DECIDES.
THE ARTIST RISES.

UNMUTE put the audience at the heart of the music industry. We connect fans directly to the artists they believe in.

On our platform, anyone can discover and support the next wave of talent. The more an artist is funded, the more visibility they gain. No algorithm decides who gets seen — the people do. Fans become the new A&Rs, choosing who rises based on pure talent. When you fund an artist, you collect points — and as more people fund them after you, your points keep growing. Use them to unlock exclusive perks: unreleased music, concert access, merch, and more.

We also host live events where emerging artists perform on stage and the audience votes for their favourites. Discover new talent in real life, support the artists you believe in, and walk away with exclusive perks.

Every artist deserves a fair chance, regardless of connections or budget.

Every contribution is traceable. Artists and fans see where the money goes.

The public is the best judge of talent.
